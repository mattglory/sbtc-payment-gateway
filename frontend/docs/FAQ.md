# Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ) 🤔

## General Platform Questions

### What is the Green Finance Platform?
The Green Finance Platform is a blockchain-powered sustainable investing platform that combines AI-driven ESG analysis, carbon footprint tracking, and sBTC payments to democratize access to green investments.

### Who can use the platform?
Anyone interested in sustainable investing can use the platform. No prior experience with cryptocurrency or ESG investing is required. We provide comprehensive guides and demo mode for learning.

### Is the platform free to use?
The platform analysis and recommendations are free. We charge small fees on investment transactions (typically 0.5-1%) to maintain and improve the service.

### How do I get started?
1. Visit the platform website
2. Enable demo mode to explore safely
3. Connect a Stacks wallet when ready to invest
4. Start with small investments to learn the system

## ESG Analysis Questions

### How accurate are the ESG scores?
Our AI-powered ESG analysis combines multiple data sources including company reports, news sentiment, regulatory filings, and third-party ESG databases. Scores are updated regularly and include confidence ratings.

### How often are ESG scores updated?
ESG scores are updated weekly for major companies and monthly for smaller companies. Breaking news or significant events may trigger immediate score updates.

### Can I analyze private companies?
Currently, our ESG analysis is limited to publicly traded companies due to data availability. We're working on expanding to private companies in the future.

### What if I disagree with an ESG score?
ESG scoring involves subjective elements. Our AI provides transparent reasoning for scores. You can always conduct additional research and make investment decisions based on your own analysis.

## sBTC and Blockchain Questions

### What is sBTC?
sBTC is a synthetic Bitcoin token on the Stacks blockchain, backed 1:1 by real Bitcoin. It enables smart contracts with Bitcoin's security while offering faster, cheaper transactions.

### Do I need to understand blockchain to use the platform?
No! Our interface handles all blockchain complexity behind the scenes. You can invest using traditional payment methods if you prefer.

### Is sBTC safe?
sBTC inherits Bitcoin's security model and is backed by real Bitcoin held in trust. Transactions are recorded on an immutable blockchain for transparency.

### What if I don't have sBTC?
You can use traditional payment methods (credit card, bank transfer) or purchase sBTC on supported exchanges like Coinbase or Binance.

## Carbon Tracking Questions

### How is my carbon footprint calculated?
We use EPA and IPCC methodologies considering transportation, energy use, consumption habits, and investment portfolio emissions. Calculations are updated annually.

### Are carbon offsets legitimate?
We only offer verified carbon offsets from Gold Standard and VCS certified projects. All offsets are real, additional, permanent, and independently verified.

### How do I become carbon neutral?
1. Calculate your annual carbon footprint
2. Purchase offsets equal to your emissions
3. Consider reducing emissions through lifestyle changes
4. Monitor and maintain carbon neutrality annually

### Can I see the specific offset projects?
Yes! Each offset purchase includes detailed information about the project, location, verification standards, and expected impact.
