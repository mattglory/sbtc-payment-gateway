## 🚀 Getting Started

### First Time Setup

1. **Access the Platform**
   - Open your web browser and navigate to the Green Finance Platform
   - The platform works on desktop, tablet, and mobile devices
   - For best experience, use Chrome, Firefox, Safari, or Edge

2. **Connect Your Wallet**
   - Click the "Connect Wallet" button in the top navigation
   - Select your preferred Stacks wallet (Hiro Wallet recommended)
   - Follow the prompts to connect and authorize the platform
   - Your wallet address will appear in the navigation bar

3. **Enable Demo Mode (Optional)**
   - Toggle the "Demo" switch in the navigation for safe exploration
   - Demo mode uses simulated data and prevents real transactions
   - Perfect for learning the platform without financial risk

### Navigation Overview

The platform features an intuitive navigation system:

- **Dashboard**: Main overview with key metrics and insights
- **ESG Analysis**: Company sustainability scoring and analysis
- **AI Recommendations**: Personalized investment suggestions
- **Carbon Tracker**: Environmental impact monitoring
- **Green Investments**: Sustainable investment opportunities
- **Payment Demo**: sBTC payment functionality

### Understanding Your Metrics

#### ESG Scores
- **A+ (90-100)**: Exceptional sustainability practices
- **A (80-89)**: Strong ESG performance
- **B+ (70-79)**: Good sustainability efforts

- **Goal**: Achieve carbon neutrality (net zero or positive)

## 🎯 ESG Analysis

The ESG Analysis tool provides comprehensive sustainability assessments for any publicly traded company.

### How to Use ESG Analysis

1. **Enter Company Information**
   - Company name or stock ticker symbol
   - Select the appropriate sector from dropdown
   - Choose geographic region (optional)

2. **Start Analysis**
   - Click "Analyze Company" button
   - AI analysis takes 20-40 seconds
   - Progress indicator shows analysis stages

3. **Review Results**
   - Overall ESG score and letter grade
   - Breakdown by Environmental, Social, and Governance factors
   - Detailed strengths and weaknesses analysis
   - Specific improvement recommendations

### Understanding ESG Scores

#### Environmental (E) Factors
- Carbon emissions and climate impact
- Renewable energy usage
- Waste management and circular economy
- Water conservation and pollution
- Biodiversity and ecosystem protection

#### Social (S) Factors
- Employee welfare and diversity
- Community impact and relations
- Customer privacy and data protection
- Supply chain labor practices
- Product safety and quality

#### Governance (G) Factors
- Board composition and independence
- Executive compensation alignment
- Transparency and reporting
- Business ethics and compliance
- Shareholder rights protection

### Using Analysis Results

#### For Investment Decisions
- Compare ESG scores across companies in your sector of interest
- Look for companies with improving trends
- Consider both current scores and improvement potential
- Balance ESG performance with financial returns

#### For Portfolio Optimization
- Identify underperforming ESG holdings
- Find replacement opportunities with better sustainability
- Maintain sector diversification while improving ESG profile
- Set ESG score targets for your portfolio

## 🤖 AI Investment Recommendations

Our AI-powered recommendation engine provides personalized investment suggestions based on your preferences and goals.

### Setting Up Preferences

1. **Risk Tolerance**
   - **Conservative**: Low volatility, stable returns, minimal risk
   - **Moderate**: Balanced approach with medium risk/reward
   - **Aggressive**: Higher volatility for potentially higher returns

2. **Investment Amount**
   - Minimum: $50 for ETF investments
   - Maximum: No upper limit
   - Recommended: $1,000+ for proper diversification

3. **Sustainability Focus**
   - Renewable Energy
   - Clean Technology
   - Sustainable Agriculture
   - Water Conservation
   - Green Buildings
   - Electric Vehicles
   - Circular Economy

4. **Investment Timeframe**
   - Short-term (1-3 years)
   - Medium-term (3-10 years)
   - Long-term (10+ years)

### Understanding Recommendations

Each recommendation includes:

#### Investment Details
- **Name and Symbol**: Company or fund identification
- **Type**: Stock, ETF, Mutual Fund, or Bond
- **Sector**: Primary business focus area
- **Minimum Investment**: Required initial amount

#### Performance Metrics
- **Expected Return**: Projected annual return percentage
- **Risk Level**: Low, medium, or high volatility
- **ESG Score**: Sustainability rating (0-100)
- **Match Score**: How well it fits your preferences (0-100)

#### Impact Metrics
- **Carbon Impact**: CO2 tons saved per $1,000 invested
- **Sustainability Focus**: Specific environmental/social benefits
- **Description**: Detailed explanation of the investment

### Best Practices

#### Diversification
- Don't invest everything in one recommendation
- Spread investments across different sectors
- Mix low, medium, and high-risk investments
- Consider geographic diversification

#### Regular Review
- Check recommendations monthly
- Rebalance portfolio quarterly
- Update preferences as goals change
- Monitor performance against benchmarks

## 🌍 Carbon Footprint Tracking

Track and offset your environmental impact through comprehensive carbon footprint monitoring.

### Understanding Your Carbon Footprint

#### Sources of Emissions
1. **Transportation**: Car, plane, train, and public transit usage
2. **Energy Consumption**: Home electricity, heating, and cooling
3. **Investment Portfolio**: Carbon footprint of your holdings
4. **Consumer Goods**: Purchases and lifestyle choices
5. **Food & Diet**: Agricultural impact of food choices

#### Calculation Methodology
- Based on EPA and IPCC guidelines
- Uses average emission factors for activities
- Includes direct and indirect emissions
- Updated annually for accuracy

### Carbon Offset Marketplace

#### Types of Offset Projects
1. **Renewable Energy**: Solar and wind farm development
2. **Reforestation**: Tree planting and forest conservation
3. **Carbon Capture**: Direct air capture and storage
4. **Clean Cookstoves**: Reducing deforestation in developing countries
5. **Methane Capture**: Landfill and agricultural gas capture

#### Verification Standards
- **Gold Standard**: Highest quality offset projects
- **Verified Carbon Standard (VCS)**: Widely accepted certification
- **Climate Action Reserve**: North American offset projects
- **Plan Vivo**: Community-focused projects

### Setting Carbon Goals

#### Carbon Neutrality
- Calculate your total annual footprint
- Purchase offsets equal to your emissions
- Achieve net-zero carbon impact

#### Carbon Negative
- Offset more than your annual emissions
- Contribute to global carbon reduction
- Support additional environmental projects

## ₿ sBTC Payment System

The platform integrates with the Stacks blockchain to enable secure sBTC payments for investments.

### What is sBTC?

#### Bitcoin Integration
- sBTC is a synthetic Bitcoin on the Stacks blockchain
- Backed 1:1 by real Bitcoin held in trust
- Enables smart contracts with Bitcoin security
- Faster and cheaper than mainnet Bitcoin transactions

#### Benefits for Green Finance
- **Security**: Bitcoin's proven security model
- **Transparency**: All transactions publicly verifiable
- **Lower Fees**: More cost-effective than traditional finance
- **Global Access**: Available worldwide 24/7

### Using sBTC Payments

#### Wallet Setup
1. **Install Stacks Wallet**: Download Hiro Wallet or compatible wallet
2. **Create Account**: Set up new wallet or import existing one
3. **Fund with sBTC**: Purchase sBTC on supported exchanges
4. **Connect to Platform**: Authorize wallet connection

#### Making Payments
1. **Select Investment**: Choose green investment opportunity
2. **Review Details**: Confirm amount and payment details
3. **Authorize Payment**: Sign transaction in wallet
4. **Confirmation**: Receive blockchain confirmation
5. **Investment Complete**: Shares appear in portfolio

#### Transaction Security
- **Multi-signature**: Enhanced security for large transactions
- **Smart Contracts**: Automated execution reduces errors
- **Immutable Records**: Permanent blockchain history
- **Decentralized**: No single point of failure

### Traditional Payment Options

For users without cryptocurrency:

#### Credit/Debit Cards
- Visa, Mastercard, American Express accepted
- Instant processing for most transactions
- Standard payment processor fees apply
- Bank-level security protocols

#### Bank Transfers
- ACH transfers for US accounts
- SEPA transfers for European accounts
- Wire transfers for large amounts
- 1-3 business day processing time

#### Digital Wallets
- PayPal, Apple Pay, Google Pay
- Quick checkout process
- Existing account integration
- Standard digital wallet fees

## 🔧 Troubleshooting

Common issues and solutions for optimal platform experience.

### Connection Issues

#### Wallet Connection Problems
**Problem**: Can't connect Stacks wallet
**Solutions**:
- Ensure wallet extension is installed and updated
- Clear browser cache and cookies
- Disable ad blockers temporarily
- Try incognito/private browsing mode
- Check wallet is unlocked

#### Slow Loading Times
**Problem**: Platform loads slowly
**Solutions**:
- Check internet connection speed
- Clear browser cache
- Disable unnecessary browser extensions
- Try different browser
- Use demo mode if live data is slow

### Transaction Issues

#### Payment Failures
**Problem**: sBTC payment doesn't process
**Solutions**:
- Verify sufficient sBTC balance
- Check wallet connection
- Increase gas fees for faster processing
- Wait for network congestion to clear
- Contact support with transaction ID

#### Investment Not Appearing
**Problem**: Completed investment doesn't show in portfolio
**Solutions**:
- Wait for blockchain confirmation (up to 10 minutes)
- Refresh page and check again
- Verify transaction on Stacks explorer
- Check if using correct wallet address
- Contact support if issue persists

### Analysis Issues

#### ESG Analysis Fails
**Problem**: Company analysis doesn't complete
**Solutions**:
- Verify company name spelling
- Try stock ticker symbol instead
- Check if company is publicly traded
- Wait and retry (may be API issue)
- Report issue to support team

#### Slow AI Recommendations
**Problem**: Investment recommendations take too long
**Solutions**:
- Check internet connection
- Reduce number of preferences selected
- Try during off-peak hours
- Clear browser cache
- Switch to demo mode temporarily

### Account Issues

#### Data Not Saving
**Problem**: Preferences or portfolio data not persisting
**Solutions**:
- Enable browser cookies
- Check local storage permissions
- Don't use incognito mode for regular use
- Update browser to latest version
- Clear cache and re-enter data

#### Missing Features
**Problem**: Some platform features not visible
**Solutions**:
- Update browser to latest version
- Enable JavaScript
- Check screen resolution (mobile vs desktop view)
- Disable ad blockers
- Try different browser

### Getting Help

#### Self-Service Options
